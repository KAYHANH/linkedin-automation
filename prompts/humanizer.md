## Principles

### 1. Eliminating AI Tells
- **Banned Clichés**:
  `delve`, `tapestry`, `realm`, `revolutionize`, `game-changer`, `seamless`, `streamline`, `testament`, `elevate`, `multifaceted`, `holistic`.
- **Banned Reveal Bridges**:
  `"Here's why:"`, `"Plot twist:"`, `"The result?"`, `"Here's the kicker:"`, `"Let that sink in"`.
- **Banned False Vulnerability**:
  `"I'll be honest"`, `"Full transparency"`, `"Confession:"`, `"Can I be real for a second"`.
- **Banned Negative Parallelism**:
  `"It's not X, it's Y"`, `"Stop doing X, start doing Y"`.

### 2. Format & Rhythm Controls
- **Em-Dash Density**: Cap at 1 per ~100 words (maximum 2 per entire post). Replace surplus em-dashes with natural commas or colons.
- **Paragraph Vocabulary Density**: AI vocabulary density per paragraph must not exceed 3.0%.
- **Zero External Links in Body**: Move all hyperlinks out of the post body to preserve LinkedIn distribution.
