R.I.P. the 40-sheet Excel workbook. It’s finally dying.

Most independent travel agencies still run their entire back office on fragmented messaging threads and brittle spreadsheets.

This setup creates a specific type of operational debt. When a supplier updates a hotel rate or a flight change hits, that change lives in a WhatsApp chat, an email, and a cell in column Q.

Reconciling these sources takes hours. It also guarantees errors.

When you are coordinating multi-vendor itineraries, a single missed update can mean a client arrives at a hotel that isn’t booked. The cost of that error isn’t just a refund. It is the loss of trust with a high-value client.

I made an unglamorous bet early on. I decided to solve this dull, back-office headache rather than chase the latest AI hype cycle.

Building software for non-technical operators is hard. You aren’t just writing code. You are mapping the messy reality of how a boutique specialist actually works.

At Viato, we focus on centralizing preferences, quotes, and supplier communications. The goal is simple. Eliminate the manual copy-paste loop. Give the specialist a single source of truth.

This isn’t about replacing the human element of travel. It’s about removing the friction that steals their time.

When the data is structured, the workflow becomes predictable. You can see the status of every vendor in real-time. You stop chasing ghosts in old email chains.

The software doesn’t need to be flashy. It needs to be reliable. It needs to handle the dynamic nature of bookings without breaking.

For founders, this is a lesson in product-market fit. Sometimes the biggest value isn’t in a new feature. It’s in cleaning up the data infrastructure that everyone else ignores.

What is the most painful manual process in your current operational stack that you wish would just disappear? #travelt #saas
